Scope and boundaries

Clear before work begins.

AI Foundations establishes shared capability within the chosen AI environment.

It does not include a production workflow with external triggers, system connections, actions, formal escalations or complex exception handling. That belongs in AI Workflow.

Platform subscriptions and other supplier charges are separate unless included in the proposal.

Common questions

Things you may be wondering.

Is this only platform setup?

No. Setup is one part. The service also establishes shared business context, reusable skills, controls and practical adoption.

Is this only training?

No. Training uses the configured environment, approved business context and reusable ways of working created through the engagement.

Does this include connected business workflows?

No. Employee used skills and starter agents are included. Production processes with external triggers, systems, actions, approvals and exceptions belong in AI Workflow.

Do we need AI Foundations before AI Workflow?

Not always. We confirm whether the necessary workspace, access, knowledge and controls already exist or should be established first.
