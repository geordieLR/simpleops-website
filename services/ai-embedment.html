Scope and boundaries

Clear before work begins.

AI Embedment creates foundations within the chosen AI environment.

It does not include a production workflow with external triggers, system connections, automated decisions, formal escalations or complex exception handling. That belongs in Agent Workflow.

Platform subscriptions and other supplier charges are separate unless included in the proposal.

Common questions

Things you may be wondering.

Is this only platform setup?

No. Setup is one part. The service also identifies useful candidates, creates reusable skills and starter agents, and supports employee adoption.

Will every employee receive training?

The service is designed for the employees included in the agreed scope. We confirm attendance and delivery arrangements before work begins.

Does this include automated business workflows?

No. Employee used skills and starter agents are included. Production workflows with external systems, triggers, decisions, reviews and escalations belong in Agent Workflow.

Do we have to buy expensive software?

No. We use the chosen environment and identify recurring costs clearly. Additional subscriptions require your approval.
