Development transparency

AI may help build it. That does not make it AI powered.

AI assisted design and development may help us produce and review code efficiently. This is often described as vibe coding.

We remain responsible for review, testing, security decisions, documentation and maintainability within the agreed scope. The application is only described as AI powered when AI is genuinely part of the delivered customer capability.

Common questions

Things you may be wondering.

Will the application be AI powered?

Only if AI is needed for the customer outcome. AI may assist development without becoming part of the finished application.

Do you always build from scratch?

No. We prefer suitable existing software and reusable components when they reduce cost, risk and ongoing maintenance.

Will we need expensive monthly software?

Not by default. We actively consider free and low cost options. Every recurring cost is shown before you approve it.

What happens after launch?

The proposal states the included support, ownership, hosting, maintenance and options for future changes.
