What the service does

Business Review in practice.

We examine how ordinary work moves through the business. That includes the people involved, the information they need, the tools they use, the decisions they make and the points where work slows down or goes wrong.

Technology may be part of the answer, but it is not the starting assumption.

Common questions

Things you may be wondering.

Is this an AI review?

No. We review the business problem and the work first. AI may appear as one possible response, alongside process, role, information or software changes.

Will employees need to prepare presentations?

No. We want to understand normal work. Existing examples and straightforward conversations are usually more useful.

Do we need to know the exact problem?

No. The review is designed for situations where the symptoms are visible but the cause or priority is unclear.

What happens after the review?

You can act on the findings yourself, engage another provider, or ask Simple Ops to help through a Sprint, Roadmap or relevant Just Happens service.
