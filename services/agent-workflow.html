Common questions

Things you may be wondering.

Is this a fully automated AI employee?

No. It is a defined production workflow with explicit responsibilities, controls, human reviews and escalations.

Do we need AI Embedment first?

Not always. We confirm whether the necessary foundations already exist or should be included before the workflow is built.

Can the workflow connect to our systems?

Often, yes. Feasibility depends on available interfaces, permissions, data quality and the controls required. We confirm this during scoping.

What happens when the agent is uncertain?

The workflow pauses, requests review or escalates according to agreed rules. Uncertainty is handled deliberately, not hidden.
