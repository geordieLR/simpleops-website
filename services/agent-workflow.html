Common questions

Things you may be wondering.

Is this a fully automated AI employee?

No. It is a defined production workflow with explicit responsibilities, controls, human approvals and escalations.

Do we need AI Foundations first?

Not always. We confirm whether the necessary workspace, knowledge, access and controls already exist or should be established first.

Does every step use AI?

No. AI is used only where flexible handling adds value. Predictable steps can use ordinary rules or automation.

What happens when the workflow is uncertain?

It pauses, requests review or escalates according to agreed rules. Uncertainty is handled deliberately, not hidden.
