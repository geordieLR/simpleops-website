Scope and boundaries

Clear before work begins.

A Sprint addresses one defined operational improvement. It is not an open programme of work.

If the main deliverable is an AI environment, production agent workflow or custom application, we recommend the relevant Just Happens service.

Licences, external suppliers and material purchases are identified and approved separately.

Common questions

Things you may be wondering.

Do we need a Business Review first?

Not always. If the problem and intended outcome are already clear, a Sprint may be the right starting point.

What can fit into one Sprint?

One contained improvement that can be designed, implemented and tested within the agreed time and budget. We narrow the scope before work begins.

Will employees test the change?

Yes. Practical testing and adoption are part of the service, provided the relevant people are available.

What if we uncover a larger issue?

We protect the Sprint outcome, record the wider issue and recommend whether it belongs in a separate Sprint or Roadmap.
