AI Advisory

Identify where AI can create practical value, choose the right starting point and implement useful change with clear ownership and controls.

This proposed engagement begins with the business and the work. It examines friction, opportunity, risk and readiness before recommending AI, ordinary automation, process improvement or no technology change.

Proposed outputs

  • Business pain points and AI opportunity map
  • Value, effort, risk and readiness priorities
  • Responsible use and human approval controls
  • AI adoption roadmap and implementation scope
  • Baseline measures and review plan

Provider approval and any funding eligibility are determined by the Regional Business Partner Network.
