Why Simple Ops exists.

Businesses rarely set out to become complicated. Complexity builds gradually, until running the business becomes harder than it needs to be.

Complexity builds gradually.

A spreadsheet solves today’s problem. Another system gets added. A temporary workaround becomes the normal process. Different people develop different ways of doing the same work. Technology gets introduced without addressing the underlying problem.

Eventually, running the business becomes harder than it needs to be.

Simple Ops exists to help change that.

Built from doing the work.

The thinking behind Simple Ops comes from experience across operations, product, technology, customer experience and commercial leadership.

It includes frontline operations, business ownership, team leadership, product portfolios, software delivery, technology strategy and business change across different industries and countries.

That breadth matters because operational problems rarely belong to one discipline. A customer problem can become a process problem. A process problem can become an information problem. An information problem can be mistaken for a technology problem.

Simple Ops looks at the whole business before deciding what should change.

A practical point of view.

The people doing the work often understand the problems better than anyone else.

They know which tools create frustration, where information disappears, which tasks are repeated and where customers experience unnecessary delays.

What they often lack is the time, support or authority to make the change happen. They may also lack the technical expertise needed to turn what they know into a workable solution.

Simple Ops starts by listening to those people, observing how the work actually happens and identifying what is most worth improving.

We translate what people describe in everyday language into clear process, information and technical requirements, then translate the options back into language the business can understand and act on.

Technology must earn its place.

Simple Ops is not a technology consultancy looking for somewhere to put technology.

Sometimes the answer is AI, automation or custom software. Sometimes it is a clearer process, better information or more effective use of tools the business already has.

The right solution is the smallest useful change that makes the work simpler, clearer or more reliable.

Why now.

Modern tools and AI have changed what smaller businesses can achieve.

Improvements that once required expensive software projects, large consulting teams or lengthy transformation programmes can now be delivered more quickly and proportionately.

But access to more technology does not automatically make a business simpler.

Simple Ops combines practical business experience, disciplined problem solving and modern delivery capability to help businesses benefit from that change, while identifying and reducing unnecessary layers of complexity.
